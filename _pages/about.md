AI engineer at VaconAI. I work on multi-agent systems, autonomous LLM pipelines, and AI products.

Most of what I build is infrastructure for AI to operate on its own. The last year or so has been: an orchestrator that takes a plain-English project description and produces a working codebase by coordinating six specialized agents, bots that run content pipelines and ML experiments without anyone watching, knowledge graph pipelines that pull structured data out of legal depositions. The thing I keep coming back to is keeping Python dumb and putting logic somewhere the LLM can read and update itself.

I've also shipped actual products — property management platforms, marketplace listing generators, a content pipeline that posts to six social channels. Before VaconAI I was at Techomnis doing more traditional stuff. Internal tools, transport systems, data pipelines. Less interesting but probably where the useful habits got formed.

Outside work I keep a Letterboxd I update more religiously than I should, have a retro game backlog I'm slowly losing the war against, and play osu when I need to stop thinking.
