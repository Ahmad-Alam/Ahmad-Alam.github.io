AI/ML engineer with production experience shipping autonomous agent systems, real-time voice AI, and ML classifiers, end to end. Currently open to new opportunities.

Most of what I build is infrastructure for AI to operate on its own. At VaconAI that was a knowledge-graph service spanning three databases (Neo4j, MongoDB, Pinecone) that turns legal deposition transcripts into queryable contradiction and admission graphs, a 10-agent LLM routing system that automates PayPal dispute resolution, and a small-talk filter for voice agents that reached 100% task recall at 12.7ms inference. Before that, at Arbisoft, I built a six-phase autonomous content pipeline that shipped 364+ verified-live articles across 13 sites, largely unattended, by replacing hardcoded Python systems with a Discord-controlled agent that reads its own operating rules from markdown instead of code. The thing I keep coming back to is keeping Python dumb and putting logic somewhere the LLM can read and update itself.

I've also shipped more traditional products — the core of a medical transport management system handling 500+ daily trips at Techomnis, plus the reporting dashboards, CSV pipelines, and backend APIs around it. Less interesting but probably where the useful habits got formed.

Outside work I keep a Letterboxd I update more religiously than I should, have a retro game backlog I'm slowly losing the war against, and play osu when I need to stop thinking.
